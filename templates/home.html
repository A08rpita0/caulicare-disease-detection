What CauliCare does

Detect, explain, compare, measure

Every photo is checked first: only clear photos of cauliflower leaves reach the disease model. Anything else, from a cat to a screenshot, is refused instead of being forced into a disease class.

{{ icon('alert') }} Responsible use

Limitations

CauliCare is an image-based screening and learning tool. It does not replace inspection by an agronomist or a laboratory test.

  • {{ icon('x') }}Trained on {{ m.dataset_images }} photos from one public dataset; real field photos can differ.
  • {{ icon('x') }}Only four classes. Other diseases or nutrient problems will be mapped to one of them.
  • {{ icon('x') }}The cauliflower-leaf check was tested on public datasets, not on real uploads; leaves of close relatives such as cabbage or broccoli may be accepted.
  • {{ icon('x') }}Bacterial spot rot and Downy Mildew are frequently confused.
